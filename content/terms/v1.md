# TERMS & CONDITIONS FOR PROVISIONAL ALLOTMENT

## General Instructions:

The price payable for the unit as mentioned overleaf is including the security deposit payable to
the T.N.E.B. for the individual metered electricity supply to the unit, Registration charge, GST,
Further, if the Government or statutory bodies involved introduce / increase later any
Tax/Levies/Charges relating to the unit, retrospectively or prospectively, the same shall become
payable by the applicant/s. The applicant/s name/s as mentioned in the Application form will be
mentioned in all the future agreements/correspondences. Hence the applicants should fill the
correct name(s) in the Application Form. No alterations/changes can be entertained at a later
date. At no point of Time, the applicant/s will be allowed to transfer the allotted unit to any
person (even close relatives) other than the applicant/s, till the date of handing over. Transfer
of ownership of undivided share in land shall be conveyed only after receiving minimum 50% of the
cost; in any case a transfer of title absolutely is possible only after full and final payment for
constructions as per agreed stage payments.

## Agreement of Sale Cum Construction:

After the date of booking applicant/s are expected to enter into Sale cum construction agreement
within 10 days, along with the particular stage payment, by way of Cheque/Online, which is subject
to realization by the Company's banker. Sale cum construction agreement is standard for all
applicant/s, no changes for particular allottee will be entertained. In case the applicant/s does
not enter into the said agreement/failures to adhere to the payment terms will entitle for
cancellation of allotment without prior intimation. Refund will be made by the Company after
deducting 5% of cancellation charges and only after the said unit is allotted to a new applicant/s.

## Arrangement of Finance / Loans:

The payment terms agreed by the applicant/s for the flat have been detailed in the provisional
allotment. If the applicant/s is depending on their employer and or any other financial
institution for loan, the applicant should contact their respective employer and / or any
financial institution not later than 7 days from the date of booking to ascertain their
eligibility. Please make sure that the payment schedule which applicant has agreed with the
Company is acceptable to them. Otherwise, applicant/s will have to make alternative arrangement to
make the payment as per the schedule. To obtain speedy approval of loans, Company has tied up with
some of the major banks/financial institutions which will be intimated to applicant/s along with
this Provisional allotment. It is entirely applicant/s decision to avail loans from the said
banks/financial institutions after thoroughly verifying their terms & conditions and interest
rates. Company would not be held responsible in any way for any delay / hike in interest rates/
change of processes /additional registration processes or any amounts payable for availing the
same /or any other issues which may arise during the loan processes.

## Payment Terms & Conditions:

For payments made through Cheque/Demand /Online, the date of clearing of the Cheque in Company's
bank account will be the date of payment. In case of outstation Cheque, the bank charges will be
deducted. The customers will be reminded 10 days prior to the subsequent payment due dates. The
customers will honour  each and every stage of scheduled payment with respect to the progress of
construction as per the payment schedule. Five days grace period from the due date of payment
shall be granted. Payment received beyond 5 days after the stages of construction duly intimated
will attract interest @ 18% p.a. calculated from the committed intimated date on the outstanding
amount. Failures to adhere to the payment terms beyond a period of one month from the due date,
will entitle for cancellation of the Provisional Allotment Cancellation charge of 5% will be
deducted from the Total sale price and Refund will be made by the Company only after having
allotted the said flat to a new buyer or 90 days, whichever is later. Dishonouring of cheques in
any manner by the attottee/s shall automatically attract the provisions of cancellation of
provisional allotment.

## Standard Feature & Additional Features:

If, the applicant/s wishes to have some additional changes made in the features offered, our
customer service department will get in touch with applicant/s, in due course in this regard.
Company will not however entertain (i) Changes that require alteration of structural design (ii)
changes that are not allowed by the rules and regulations of Corporations / Local Bodies, CMDA or
any Statutory Bodies (iii) changes that affect the building and (iv) changes that involve
encroachment into common areas and setbacks. Architectural features like Elevation, Colour /
Colour combination or any other feature affecting aesthetics of the building, shown in the
marketing brochures are indicative only. All the specifications mentioned in the brochures are
only Indicative and at any point of time, the company may change without giving any intimation.
The company's decision / interpretation in the above matters will be final and binding and also
company reserves the right to change the same.

## Work Availability:

To meet bulk needs of the complex Company will provide one or more captive sources of water in
form of open wells and or wells, which in Company's assessment will meet the needs of the complex
other than for drinking and cooking. These sources may be new or may have already existed on the
property. However, Company cannot give a guarantee on either the portability or the perennial
availability of water from these sources.

## Contribution to the Unit Owner's Association:

After the Handing over, all the allottees of the flat shall form a Society of Flat Owners
Association, the applicant/s has no right to deny his/her membership and abide by its rules and
regulations as and when framed. The applicant/s agrees and consents that any decision as to
contribution of amount, maintenance, repair, etc., taken/imposed by such society shall be binding
and agreeable on the applicant/s.

## Withdrawal from the Project:

Due to some unforeseen circumstances, if the applicant/s have to withdraw from the project, he/she
has to submit the written application for cancellation along with all the original documents and
copies that were given to them till the date of cancellation and agree for the forfeiture of 5%of
the Total Unit Cost from the advance payment made. Refund will be made by the Company only after
having allotted the said unit to a new buyer or 90days, whichever is later.

I hereby agreed and accepted the above-mentioned terms & condition of this provisional allotment.
