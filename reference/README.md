| `booking-form.jpeg` | Page 1 — the BOOKING FORM sheet |

Drop the two photos in this folder, keeping exactly these names.

For the best result — the PDF has to be a visual match, and the T&C text is legally binding:

- Shoot straight down on a flat sheet, in even light, no shadow across the page.
- Fill the frame with the sheet; get all four corners in.
- Make sure the small print under the section headings is legible when zoomed in. If the T&C body
  text is not sharp, a flatbed scan at 300 dpi (or a second close-up photo per section) is far
  better than one wide shot.
- Pasting the T&C text into `/content/terms/v1.md` instead removes the transcription risk
  entirely — strongly preferred.
